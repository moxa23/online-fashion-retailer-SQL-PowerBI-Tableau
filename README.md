Urban Loom is a UK-based online fashion retailer founded by designer Jo Grey, specialising in casual unisex clothing available in 23 colours and 9 sizes. Established in January, the brand focuses on comfort and inclusivity, with plans to open a physical store in the UK.

Our team used SQL, Power BI, and Tableau to analyse business data, developing three KPIs for each of the three core business questions. Through interactive dashboards and visual storytelling, we uncovered key insights and delivered data-driven recommendations.

The project was recognised for its clarity and strategic impact, presenting future AI/ML opportunities and a long-term data strategy that addressed governance, privacy, and ethics.
